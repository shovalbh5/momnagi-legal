Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

MOMNAGI ("we", "the app") is a pregnancy-to-parenting companion. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Account: email address and sign-in identifier (email, Sign in with Apple, or Google).
  • Profile and tracking data you enter: pregnancy week or child details, check-ins, logs (e.g. feeding, sleep, contractions), journal entries and preferences.
  • Community content: posts and comments you submit, which are moderated and visible to other users (anonymous option available).
  • AI chat messages you send to the in-app assistant.
  • Purchase status (via RevenueCat and Apple); we never see your payment details.
  • Push notification token, only if you enable notifications.

How we use it

To run the app, personalize guidance, moderate the community, process subscriptions and send notifications you opted into. We do not sell your data and do not use it for third-party advertising or tracking.

Third-party processors

  • Supabase – database and authentication hosting.
  • Anthropic (Claude) – when you use AI chat, translation or community moderation, the relevant text is sent to Anthropic to generate a response. You are asked for consent before first use of AI chat.
  • RevenueCat / Apple – subscription management.
  • Expo – push notification delivery.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and all associated data any time in Settings → Privacy & Data.
  • Export or request your data by contacting us.
  • Report or block other users in the community.

Retention and security

Data is kept while your account is active and deleted when you delete your account. Data is encrypted in transit and access is restricted by row-level security.

Not medical advice

MOMNAGI provides general information and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, contact your local emergency number.

Children

MOMNAGI is intended for adults (18+). Information about a child is entered by their parent or guardian.
